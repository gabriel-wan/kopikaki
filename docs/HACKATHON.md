About Event
​Join us on August 22, 2026, for Build with Gemini, a full-day hackathon hosted with Lorong AI.

​We've got three tracks for you to compete for.

​Best Use Of Gemma : Our Gemma models have just crossed 1bn total consecutive downloads. Push the limits of what an Open Model can do within the limited time that you have

​Best Elderly Hack : Nearly 1 in 3 people will be seniors by 2030. Modern tech isn't built for the elderly in mind so build something that makes it easier for them to stay connected.

​Most Creative Gemini Hack : We've just dropped Gemini Flash 3.6. What's the wildest, fastest, or most creative way you can put it to work?

​Stand to walk away with cash prizes for each track! Limited slots available, RSVP today :)
